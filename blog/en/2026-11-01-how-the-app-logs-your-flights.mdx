Filling in a logbook means copying times that the iPhone in your pocket already saw go by. The PilotDuty app starts from that idea: it records the steps of the flight while you fly, and all that's left is to review and approve.

## It all starts with a zone

The app doesn't follow all your movements. It watches the zones you choose: an airfield, or an aircraft fitted with an iBeacon. When the iPhone enters one of these zones, tracking starts. When it leaves, tracking stops.

With a beacon on board, the app knows which aircraft you're in. It also checks that the beacon is really there before accepting a takeoff: a car ride along the runway doesn't become a flight.

## Speed tells the story

Inside a zone, the app follows the iPhone's GPS speed, smoothed over several readings, and the motion the iPhone detects. When the aircraft starts to roll, that's off-block time. When speed passes a threshold set from your aircraft's takeoff speed, that's takeoff. Dropping below a lower threshold marks the landing, and the stop gives on-block time.

All of this works without a network: the flight is kept on the iPhone.

## Every time has its confidence score

An iPhone's GPS isn't always precise. When the flight ends, the app rates how reliable each time and both airports are, in green, yellow or red. When GPS missed a key moment, it suggests an estimated time, with its margin. Nothing is replaced without your say.

With PilotDuty Pro, if a flight ends without a usable GPS track, the app can also find its legs in public ADS-B data and offer them to you as a draft.

## You have the last word

The detected flight arrives as a draft on the dashboard. You review it, fix what needs fixing, then one swipe approves it. Only then does it enter your logbook and, if you're a member of an organization, appear in PilotDuty Manager.

## Getting started

The app is free for up to 3 flights a month. The [Automatic recording](/en/docs/app/automatic-recording) guide explains how to set up your zones, and the [iBeacon](/en/docs/app/ibeacon) guide how to install a beacon.
