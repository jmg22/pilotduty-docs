Duty time is a PilotDuty Pro feature, in beta. The app compares your duty, flight and rest periods with the limits of the regulation you choose (CARs, FAR Part 135, EASA ORO.FTL or your own template).

It's a helper tool: it doesn't replace your own check, and you remain responsible for complying with your regulation. In PilotDuty Manager, duty stats are indicative guides: each total sits next to a fixed Canadian limit, taken from the 702 and 703 rules, with no warning or blocking.
