No. The two subscriptions are separate. The organization's subscription removes the 3-flights-a-month limit in the app and enables flight following in the Manager; PilotDuty+ and Pro are bought separately, in the app.

Conversely, a personal PilotDuty+ or Pro subscription doesn't pay for the organization's subscription.
