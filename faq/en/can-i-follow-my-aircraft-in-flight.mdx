question: "Can I follow my aircraft in flight?"

Yes, if your organization subscribes and the pilot turns on flight following to PilotDuty Manager in the iPhone app. The app sends the flight's milestones (taxi, takeoff, landing, parking) with their position. The Manager shows them live on the Flightsheet and its map.

Text message subscribers, who are members of the organization, receive the messages for the events they chose, including the overdue alert. Staff (Instructor and above) confirm that a pilot has landed safely and see emergency contacts. Without an iPhone sending milestones, no position is shown. See [Flight following](/en/docs/manager/flight-following).
