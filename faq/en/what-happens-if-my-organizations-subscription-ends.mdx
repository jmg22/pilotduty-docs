---
question: "What happens if my organization's subscription ends?"
category: subscriptions
order: 6
translationKey: faq-org-subscription-ends
updated: 2026-11-01
draft: false
---

If the organization has more than one member, all its members, including the Operations Manager, are sent to the subscription page. There is no grace period: a late payment has the same effect. These pages stay open: profile, organization settings, members and invitations, flight following settings, subscription, memos and “Feedback”.

To keep using the Manager for free, the organization must go back to a single member. The organization's data is kept: it can subscribe again later and find its data again. Once the subscription has ended, the Stripe customer portal button disappears: keep a copy of your invoices. See [Subscription](/en/docs/manager/subscription).
