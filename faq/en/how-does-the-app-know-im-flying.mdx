The app watches the zones you choose: an airfield, or an aircraft fitted with an iBeacon. Inside one of these zones, it follows the iPhone's GPS speed and motion to recognize taxi, takeoff, landing and stop.

The flight is saved as a draft. It only enters your logbook once you approve it. See [Automatic recording](/en/docs/app/automatic-recording).
