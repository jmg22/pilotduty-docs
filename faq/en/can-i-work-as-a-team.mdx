Yes. You invite your members by email and give each one a role, out of eleven, that decides what they see and what they can do. Staff (Instructor and above) can view pilots' files: medical, competencies, logbook and duty stats.

You can start on your own, for free. To invite a second member, the organization must be subscribed. You keep the same organization: there's nothing to transfer. See [Members and roles](/en/docs/manager/members-and-roles).
