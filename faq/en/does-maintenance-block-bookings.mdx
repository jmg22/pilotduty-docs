---
question: "Does a defect or an overdue inspection block bookings?"
category: manager
order: 3
translationKey: faq-maintenance
updated: 2026-11-01
draft: false
---

No. The maintenance module informs: it shows due items and defects, but it doesn't remove the aircraft from bookings and doesn't send due-date alerts.

To make an aircraft unavailable, enter an unavailability on the calendar. See [Fleet and maintenance](/en/docs/manager/fleet-and-maintenance).
