PilotDuty brings together an iPhone app for pilots and an optional web application, PilotDuty Manager, for organizations.

The app keeps your logbook: it records your flights on its own, gives them to you to approve and tracks your night and IFR currency. With PilotDuty+ or Pro, it also keeps someone informed of your flights by text message; with PilotDuty Pro, it tracks your duty time (beta). It's built for private and professional pilots alike, and works on its own, without an organization.

PilotDuty Manager is for aviation organizations, including aircraft owner groups. See [Getting started with the app](/en/docs/app/getting-started) and [Getting started with the Manager](/en/docs/manager/getting-started).
