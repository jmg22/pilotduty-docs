Take an annual PilotDuty+ or PilotDuty Pro subscription: the beacon is included the first year. After purchase, the app asks for your shipping address. You can also buy a beacon in the [iBeacon shop](/en/shop/ibeacon), with free shipping to Canada and the United States.

Placed in the aircraft, the beacon tells the app you are on board: flight recording starts when your iPhone picks it up. This trigger requires PilotDuty+ or PilotDuty Pro.

See [iBeacon](/en/docs/app/ibeacon) to install it and pair it with your aircraft.
