The iPhone app is free for up to 3 flights a month; PilotDuty+ and PilotDuty Pro are bought in the app. PilotDuty Manager is free, with no time limit, for a one-member organization; beyond that, the organization picks Growth or Unlimited.

PilotDuty+ adds, among other things, unlimited flights, beacon-triggered recording with an iBeacon, logbook export and import, and text message flight following. PilotDuty Pro also adds duty time, pilot health, ADS-B recovery and the airline roster. Growth and Unlimited have the same features: the Growth amount follows the number of members, the Unlimited amount is flat. See [Subscriptions](/en/docs/app/subscriptions) for the app and [Subscription](/en/docs/manager/subscription) for the Manager.

All prices, in US dollars, are on the [Pricing](/en/pricing) page.
