The iPhone app is free for up to 3 flights a month; PilotDuty+ and PilotDuty Pro are bought in the app. PilotDuty Manager is free for a one-member organization; beyond that, the organization picks Growth or Unlimited.

All prices, in US dollars, are on the [Pricing](/en/pricing) page.
