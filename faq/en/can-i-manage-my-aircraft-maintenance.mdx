Yes. The Fleet module tracks each aircraft's airframe hours and cycles, updated on their own from members' flights. You set up inspection schedules (for example 50-hour or 100-hour/annual) and items to track by hours, by date or by cycles.

An inspection generates the work order and the status report as PDFs, then the alert card when you finalize it. You can attach the work report, the inspection task and the quality control to it. Members (Pilot and above) report snags from the app or the Manager. See [Fleet and maintenance](/en/docs/manager/fleet-and-maintenance).
