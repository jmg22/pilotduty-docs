---
question: "Who can see my flights if I'm a member of an organization?"
category: account
order: 1
translationKey: faq-privacy-org
updated: 2026-11-01
draft: false
---

Your flights stay in the app until you approve them. Once approved, they're in your logbook, which your organization's authorized staff (Instructor role and above) can view in the Manager, including flights flown elsewhere.

Flight following to the Manager is a setting you turn on in the app.
