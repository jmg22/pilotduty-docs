Yes, with PilotDuty+ or Pro. The app imports CSV files from LogTen Pro, ForeFlight and MyFlightbook, with a preview and duplicate detection. A paper logbook can be photographed page by page (in beta).

You can also enter your hours from before PilotDuty as totals. See [Logbook import](/en/docs/app/logbook-import).
