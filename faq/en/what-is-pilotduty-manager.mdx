It's your organization's web application: bookings, flight sheet, flight following, maintenance, pilot hours, billing, training and memos, in one place. Pilots who use the iPhone app send their approved flights to it.

It's built for any aviation organization: aircraft rental, flight training, scenic flights, air taxi, aerial work, owner groups. See [Getting started](/en/docs/manager/getting-started).
