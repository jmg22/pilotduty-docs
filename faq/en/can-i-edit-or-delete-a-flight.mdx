---
question: "Can I edit or delete a flight?"
category: app
order: 7
translationKey: faq-edit-flight
updated: 2026-11-01
draft: false
---

Yes. Before approval, tap the flight on the dashboard to correct its times, aerodromes, aircraft or remarks. Swipe it to the right to delete it.

After approval, unapprove it from the logbook and enter the reason: it returns to the approval queue, where you correct it before approving it again. Each change creates a version, which you'll find in the flight's history.

You can also correct your flights in PilotDuty Manager: the app receives those changes. Your organization's staff can view your logbook, but can't edit it. See [Review and approval](/en/docs/app/review-and-approval).
