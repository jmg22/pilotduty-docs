The “Bookings” calendar is the shared schedule of your aircraft, crews and rooms.

## Who can do it

- **View**: every member, Read-only included.
- **Create**: Student and above (for themselves below Pilot), subject to the service's minimum role.
- **Create a series or an unavailability**: Pilot and above.
- **Edit**: Dispatch and above by default, or the assigned pilot and instructor if the service allows it.
- **Refund or void an invoice**: Chief Instructor and above.
- **Set up** (booking settings): Chief Instructor, Chief Pilot, Co-owner and Operations Manager.

## Read the calendar

- Month, week, day and agenda views (7 days). A calendar and timeline switch turns on a horizontal view, with one line per resource.
- Split by all, by aircraft, by pilot or by room, and filter by aircraft, crew member, room, service or status.
- Choose the base, the division, and local or UTC times.
- Hatched bands mark an unavailability, and a padlock marks a day locked on the Flightsheet.

## Create a booking

1. Click the add button, or click an empty slot in the calendar.
2. Choose to create a booking, add an unavailability, or block yourself.
3. Follow the assistant: service, aircraft, crew, schedule, passengers, customer, notes. From a slot, a training flight or a rental fits on a single screen.
4. At the end, check who gets a confirmation (customer, student, crew) and choose the billing: send a payment request, pay on site, or no invoice for now.

A booking created by staff is confirmed right away.

## Create a series

At the recurrence step, repeat the booking every N days, weeks, months or years, up to a number of occurrences or a date. A preview lists conflicts before you confirm. By default, a series stops at 52 occurrences. To edit a series, choose the scope: this occurrence, the following ones, or the whole series.

## Move a booking

Drag the booking to change its time or, in day or timeline view, to move it to another aircraft, pilot or room. Stretch it to change its length (rental, training…). A confirmation window offers to notify the customers and the crew.

## Conflicts are refused

The Manager refuses a booking that overlaps another one on the same aircraft, the same pilot or crew member, or in a room that is full or already taken. It also refuses a blocked aircraft or too few seats. The refusal names the resource involved. Outside the hours of the base, the service or the crew member, staff get a warning and can schedule anyway. Online, these cases are always refused.

## Statuses and approval

A booking is pending, confirmed, in a cart (online, not yet completed) or canceled. If a service requires approval, your customers' bookings stay pending. To confirm one, open it and choose confirmed in the status selector at the top.

## Cancel

Choose canceled in the status selector. For a booking with customers or a series, a guided flow handles refunds, invoices and cancellation fees, and offers to cancel all future occurrences. For a series, keep the page open until it finishes. Below Chief Instructor, uncheck the invoices to void: that operation is reserved to Chief Instructors and above. Default cancellation fees are set in the booking settings, as a percentage or a fixed amount, with a free-cancellation window.

## Unavailabilities

Adding an unavailability makes an aircraft, a person or a room unbookable. Blocking yourself makes you unavailable in two steps, handy on a phone. An unavailability applies to all bases and divisions, for one year at most.

## Reminders and notifications

The notifications tab of the booking settings manages booking emails and text messages, by recipient and by language.

- **Reminder lead time**: 1 to 72 hours before the start, 24 hours by default. The reminder goes out for a confirmed booking, if its channel is on, give or take about an hour.
- By default, the customer reminder goes out by text message and the crew reminder is off.
- The template editor customizes each message, language by language, with variables and a text-message segment counter. Every text message starts with “PilotDuty:”.
- Sending a test emails you a sample message; a text message is only previewed.

## Privacy for other members

Below Dispatch, you see in full the bookings where you are crew, student or customer. Others show as busy: names and personal details are hidden.

## Good to know

- A booking cannot be deleted: canceling is the only way.
- Only an unavailability removes an aircraft from booking: a defect or an overdue inspection does not. See [Fleet and maintenance](/en/docs/manager/fleet-and-maintenance).
