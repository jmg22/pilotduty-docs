Memos send an internal notice to your members and keep proof of who read and confirmed it.

## Who can do it

- Read your memos and confirm them as read and understood: every member, Read-only included.
- Write, publish and track a memo for the organization: Dispatch and above.
- An active trainer can write to their own students only.

## Write a memo

1. Open Memos, go to the publishing tab and create a new memo.
2. Fill in the title and the text (with a write tab and a preview tab), the category, the priority (normal, important or urgent), the effective date and, if needed, an expiry date.
3. Ask for a read-and-understood confirmation and, if needed, set a confirmation deadline.
4. Attach your files: up to 10 files, 4 MB each, in PDF, JPEG, PNG or HEIC.
5. Save the draft.

## Choose the audience

Combine as needed: all members, by role, by group, a trainer's students, or selected members. Someone targeted twice receives the memo only once. Before you publish, the recipient preview shows who will receive it and through which channel.

Groups are created under groups and categories, with up to 500 members per group. The five categories (safety, operations, maintenance, training, administration) can be renamed and recolored.

## Channels

A memo always appears in the Memos inbox, in the Manager and in the iPhone app. You can also send it by email and by push notification to the iOS app. Memos are never sent by text message.

## Publish

Publishing makes the memo official: it gets a number in the format MEMO-YYYY-NNN and can no longer be edited. It goes out as soon as it is published, even if its effective date is later.

To correct a published memo, replace it: a new version is created, and members must confirm it again. Archiving is final.

## Track who read it

The tracking section shows, name by name, who read the memo, who confirmed it, who is pending or late, and any delivery failures with their reason. The first time a member opens the memo, the read time is recorded. Read and understood records a timestamped confirmation that the member cannot undo. After the deadline, it shows as confirmed late.

Sending a reminder emails or notifies the people who have not read or confirmed the memo.

## Proof of distribution and poster

- Export to CSV or PDF for a named proof of distribution, ready for an audit. The PDF includes the fingerprint of the published content.
- Download the poster (PDF) for your bulletin board, with a QR code to the memo. The code opens the memo for a signed-in member of the organization, not for the public.

## Good to know

- Reminders are manual: nothing is sent again automatically. The same person cannot be reminded twice within 30 minutes.
- There is no public memo page and no scheduled publishing.
- Attachments are not included in the email or on the poster: only their names appear.
- The memo text stays in its author's language.
- A new member receives memos still in effect in their inbox, without email or notification.
- All members does not include Read-only members.
- Publishing requires an active subscription if the organization has more than one member. Reading is never blocked.
