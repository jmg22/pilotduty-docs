The PilotDuty iPhone app and the Manager use the same account and the same database. What a pilot does in the app shows up in the Manager, with no export or import.

## Same account, same database

Pilots sign in to the app with the account they use in the Manager. They can also use the app on their own, without an organization. To get started with the app, see the [app guide](/en/docs/app/getting-started).

## Approved flights show up

A flight recorded by the app stays on the iPhone until the pilot approves it. Once approved, it appears in their logbook, under their profile in the Manager. It updates their totals on its own, as well as the hours and cycles of the organization's aircraft. A correction made in the Manager reaches the app right away. See [Pilots and duty time](/en/docs/manager/pilots-and-duty-time).

## Flight following

In the app, the pilot turns on the setting for flight following on PilotDuty Manager. This setting is only available to members of a subscribed organization.

After takeoff, the flight appears on the flight sheet and on the map, with its milestones: position, altitude and heading. It is matched on its own to the aircraft's confirmed booking, and a crew on the same flight is grouped together. The pilot can confirm their arrival from their iPhone. See [Flight following](/en/docs/manager/flight-following).

## Aircraft records and snags

- To use an organization aircraft in the app, the pilot imports it as an organization aircraft.
- In the app, they can view the aircraft record: specifications, hours, cycles, next inspection and active snags.
- A member with the Pilot role or above can report a snag from the app. It arrives on the aircraft's snags tab in the Manager: see [Fleet and maintenance](/en/docs/manager/fleet-and-maintenance).

Aircraft and maintenance management stays in the Manager: the app views and reports.

## Memos

Memos published in the Manager arrive in the app's memo inbox, with a notification if the author chose one. The pilot reads them, opens attachments and confirms them as read and understood; you track the confirmations in the Manager. A memo link opens straight in the app. This feature does not require a personal subscription. See [Memos](/en/docs/manager/memos).

## Medical, competencies and airline schedule

The medical certificates, licences and competencies a pilot keeps in the app (PilotDuty Pro personal subscription) appear on their pilot profile in the Manager. An airline schedule source added in the app appears in the Manager, and vice versa (Pro). See [Calendars](/en/docs/manager/calendars).

## A subscribed organization lifts the flight limit

The free app is limited to 3 flights per month. A pilot who is a member of a subscribed organization no longer has this limit. The organization's subscription does not include PilotDuty+ or PilotDuty Pro: see [Subscription](/en/docs/manager/subscription).

## What the app does not do

- The app has no booking features. To see their bookings on an iPhone, members add their calendar sync link to their calendar: see [Calendars](/en/docs/manager/calendars).
- The flight sheet, billing, training, writing memos and managing the fleet all happen in the Manager.
- Regulatory duty time is calculated in the app (PilotDuty Pro personal subscription). The Manager only shows benchmarks.

## Good to know

- A flight the pilot has not approved does not exist for the Manager, except for its flight following.
- A flight only counts toward an aircraft's hours if its pilot is a member of the aircraft's organization.
- As the final screen of the aircraft wizard points out, a later change to an aircraft's configuration is not carried over to iPhones that already imported it.
