PilotDuty Manager is the web app that brings together the management of your aviation organization in one place, whatever its line of business.

## What the Manager does

- A shared calendar to book your aircraft, crews and rooms.
- An online booking widget to embed in your website: your customers book without an account.
- The Flightsheet, which matches each booked day with the flights actually flown, then freezes it in a record.
- Flight following, fed by your pilots' iPhone app.
- The fleet and its maintenance, customer records and billing through Stripe.
- Each pilot's file, the training record with electronic signature, and memos to members.

Each member has a role that decides what they can see and do. See [Members and roles](/en/docs/manager/members-and-roles).

## Create your account

Sign up with your email and a password of at least 6 characters. If you forget your password, an email link lets you choose a new one.

## Create your organization

After you sign up, the welcome screen offers two choices: create your organization or join an existing one. Creating it takes three steps.

1. **Details**: organization name, your full name (used for electronic signatures), address, phone and email. If an organization with a similar name already exists, an alert tells you so. You can still create yours.
2. **Operations**: choose at least one activity: flight training, aircraft rental, sightseeing flights, air taxi and charter, aerial work, or other. Enter your country: the civil aviation authority and the currency are filled in from it, and you can change them. Then choose your main airport: it becomes your first operating base, open from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.
3. **Done**: a screen confirms that your organization is created. Its button opens “Bookings”, where a guide lists the setup steps: operating base, aircraft, pilots and instructors, services.

You become the Operations Manager, the owner role of the organization. Later, the organization selector lets you create another organization. You can belong to several organizations, with a different role in each.

## Join an existing organization

If your organization already uses the Manager, ask for an invitation. Open the link you received by email, or paste the link or the code in the welcome screen, then join. Accept with the account whose email address was invited.

## On your own or as a team

As long as your organization has only one member, you use it free of charge, with no subscription and no time limit. To invite members, the organization needs an active subscription, and only the Operations Manager can subscribe. Beyond one member, without a subscription in good standing, the pages send you to the subscription page. See [Subscription](/en/docs/manager/subscription).

## Set up your organization

Your organization's details are set under Settings › Organization, on the general tab. Every member can see this page. Only Chief Instructors and above can change the details and settings: for other roles, these fields are grayed out.

1. Add your logo at the top of the tab. It is saved right away and appears in the organization selector, in invitation emails and on inspection PDFs.
2. Fill in the organization's name, address, phone number and email.
3. Check the regulatory authority: TC (Transport Canada), FAA (United States), EASA (Europe) or other. It sets the operating rules offered for your aircraft and flights. If you choose other, the Canadian rules apply.
4. Save your changes with the update button.

The same tab also holds the default time zone, used only when no base has a time zone, the time format (local or UTC, for the whole organization) and the automatic lock of the [Flightsheet](/en/docs/manager/flight-sheet).

## The main menu

The sidebar starts with the organization selector, then:

- **“Flightsheet”**: a base's day, flight by flight. It is the home page.
- **“Bookings”**: the shared calendar.
- **“Open Classes”**: your public ground classes, if your organization has turned on this option.
- **“Shop”**: counter sales of items, if the shop is turned on.
- **“Memos”**: messages to members.
- **“Pilots”**, **“Aircrafts”** and **“Clients”**: pilot files, the fleet and customer records.
- **“Settings”**: your profile, the organization, the subscription and booking.
- **“Feedback”**: write to the PilotDuty team.

At the bottom, the profile menu offers the theme (light, dark or system) and sign-out. On a phone, a menu icon replaces the sidebar. There is no global search, but the member, pilot and customer lists each have their own.

## Interface language

The interface is available in English, French, Spanish and German. Choose the language at the bottom of the sidebar, on a computer. The choice follows the page address: it is not saved in your profile. Several screens remain in English. The language of the notifications you receive is set separately, in your profile.

## With the iPhone app

The PilotDuty app and the Manager share the same account and the same database.

- Flight following starts in the pilot's iPhone app and comes up on the Flightsheet.
- A defect reported in the app appears in the aircraft's maintenance.
- Members read their memos in the app.
- The app has no booking feature: bookings are made in the Manager or through the widget.
- If you already used the app, creating an organization does not replace your pilot profile.

The organization's subscription and the pilot's personal App Store subscription are separate. See [With the iPhone app](/en/docs/manager/with-the-iphone-app).

## Good to know

- The airport search in the setup assistant covers Canada and the United States. Elsewhere, no base is created: add it later in the booking settings.
- The activities chosen during setup only sort the first service suggestions. They cannot be changed afterward.
