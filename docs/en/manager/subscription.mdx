The organization's subscription opens the Manager to all its members. PilotDuty Manager uses Stripe to handle subscriptions, and Stripe processes payments securely. All subscription and billing operations are under Settings › “Subscription”.

## Growth or Unlimited

If no subscription is active, the “Subscription” page shows the two plans:

- **Growth**: the amount follows the number of members in the organization.
- **Unlimited**: a flat amount, for an unlimited number of members.

Both plans have exactly the same features, including duty time benchmarks, flight following, aircraft maintenance management and unlimited events for each member. Only the way the amount is calculated differs.

- Choose **Growth** if you are a small organization just getting started.
- Choose **Unlimited** if your team is large or growing: the amount no longer changes when you add members.

Both are available monthly or yearly. For prices, see the [Pricing](/en/pricing) page.

## A one-member organization

A one-member organization uses the Manager for free, with no time limit. To invite a second member, the organization must be subscribed.

## Who can do it

Every member can see the subscription page. Only the Operations Manager can subscribe and open the Stripe customer portal. Other members see a message saying so.

## Subscribe

1. Go to Settings › “Subscription”.
2. At the top of the page, choose the billing period with the “Monthly” / “Yearly” toggle:
   - **Monthly**: you pay every month, for more flexibility;
   - **Yearly**: you pay once a year.
3. Under the plan you want, click “Checkout” to begin the subscription process.
4. A secure Stripe payment window opens. Enter your email, your credit card details, then your billing country and postal code. Click “Subscribe” to confirm your plan.
5. Once the payment is processed, the “Done! You're all set.” screen confirms your subscription. A confirmation email goes to the address entered at checkout. Click “Proceed to App” to return to PilotDuty Manager.

## View your active subscription

After subscribing, the “Subscription” tab shows a card with your plan, its status, the price and the renewal or cancellation date. The price shown is the unit price, not the total billed. Under the card, “Go to Customer Portal” opens the customer portal to manage billing.

## Growth plan seats

On the Growth plan, the billed quantity follows the number of members. It goes up when an invitee accepts their invitation and goes down when you remove a member from member management: see [Members and roles](/en/docs/manager/members-and-roles). Pending invitations do not count.

## The Stripe customer portal

The customer portal button takes you to your organization's Stripe portal. That is where the subscription's billing is managed. In the customer portal, you can, for example:

- update your payment method;
- view or download invoices;
- cancel or change your subscription plan;
- add a backup card.

Changes made in the portal come back to your PilotDuty account on their own. The Manager itself has no button to switch plans: use the portal. Once the subscription has ended, the portal button disappears: keep a copy of your invoices.

## Statuses and late payment

The subscription card shows a status badge, such as active, past due, unpaid, canceled or expired. If there is a problem, a badge also appears in the sidebar.

If the organization has more than one member and the subscription is no longer active, for example after a late payment, all members are sent to the subscription page. There is no grace period. These pages stay open: profile, organization settings, members and invitations, flight following settings, subscription, memos and “Feedback”. To keep using the Manager for free, the organization must go back to a single member. The organization's data is kept: it can subscribe again later and find its data again.

An email is sent when the organization subscribes, changes plans, cancels or reactivates. It goes to the payment method's email address, otherwise to the Stripe customer's, otherwise to the organization's. No PilotDuty email announces a failed payment: keep an eye on the status badge.

## Pilot subscriptions are separate

PilotDuty+ and PilotDuty Pro are personal subscriptions, purchased in the iPhone app through the App Store. They are separate from the organization's subscription:

- the organization's subscription does not include PilotDuty+ or PilotDuty Pro;
- a pilot subscription does not pay for the organization.

In the Manager, a personal subscription unlocks logbook import (PilotDuty+ or Pro) and airline calendar sync (Pro). The Manager does not sell them: they are managed from the PilotDuty iOS app. The organization's subscription also has effects in the app: see [With the iPhone app](/en/docs/manager/with-the-iphone-app).

## Send feedback

The “Feedback” menu entry lets you report a bug or suggest a feature, with a title and a description. Your message goes straight to the PilotDuty team, and a ticket number is displayed. The page stays open even without an active subscription. You can send up to five messages per hour, without attachments.

## Good to know

- Growth and Unlimited have the same features: only the way the amount is calculated differs.
- The subscription is paid through Stripe and managed through the Stripe customer portal, which only the Operations Manager can open.
- A late payment immediately blocks the pages for all members.
