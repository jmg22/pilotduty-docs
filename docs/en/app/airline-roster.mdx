Connecting your airline roster brings your scheduled flights into PilotDuty. You review and approve them like your other flights.

With PilotDuty Pro. This feature is in beta.

## Adding a source

You need the iCal link (an address ending in .ics) that your employer provides for your roster.

1. In the Settings tab, open Profile, then the roster sources in the logbook section. You can also choose the airline roster during the welcome flow.
2. Name the source and paste the iCal link.
3. If you know it, enter your airline's IATA code. It lets the server fill in more details for your flights.
4. Set the “Default Info”: category, engine and propeller, flight type, country and operating rule, status, and buffers before and after the flight.
5. Save.

The server reads your roster, recognizes how your airline lays it out, then prepares the flights and events for approval.

## Privacy

- Your link is encrypted on the server. The app never keeps it in plain text and only shows its last 4 characters.
- The app doesn't read your iPhone's calendar. It doesn't ask for any calendar permission.
- Only iCal links are accepted.

## Approving flights and events

Flights and events from your roster appear on the dashboard.

- Swipe left to approve, right to reject.
- Tap an item to edit it before approving: dates, time zones, acclimatization, remark and flights. Save your changes; saving requires a network.
- You can remove a single flight from an event with “Delete leg”. If that isn't possible, the app offers to reject the whole event.
- If the roster changed at the source, a banner tells you. Once you've checked, confirm it in the banner.
- A magic wand icon marks a flight the server has filled in.

These items aren't recorded live: you can't add a flight to them, stop them or pause them.

If you unapprove a flight that came from the roster, it leaves the logbook and is offered to you again from the roster.

## Managing your sources

In a source's details, you can:

- rename it;
- turn it off or back on;
- refresh it right away with “Refresh now”;
- set the past and future windows to read;
- rerun the recognition of the roster's layout;
- delete it.

A source's address can't be changed in the app. Banners flag problems, such as a missing IATA code or a layout that wasn't recognized. You can dismiss them.

## With your organization

If you belong to an organization, a source you add in the app also appears in PilotDuty Manager, and a source added in Manager appears in the app. Flights you approve show up in Manager like your other flights.
