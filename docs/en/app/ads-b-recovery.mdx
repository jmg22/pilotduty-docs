ADS-B recovery finds a flight from the positions the aircraft broadcast over ADS-B. Use it when your iPhone couldn't record the flight, or to check times.

With PilotDuty Pro.

## Recovering a flight by hand

1. Tap “+” on the dashboard, then “Recover Flight ADS-B”.
2. Choose the aircraft and the time window to search. By default, the window covers 24 hours.
3. Tap the search button. The app doesn't search on its own when you change the window.
4. The app lists the rebuilt flights. Tap the one you want.

The flight is created as a draft in the approval queue. It includes the type, registration, cycles, and whether it was day or night. It doesn't go into your logbook until you approve it.

You can also start this recovery from a duty event's screen.

## Automatic rebuild when a flight closes

When a flight ends without a usable GPS track, the app makes a note of it. When you come back to the app, or when you leave the beacon's zone, it queries ADS-B and creates draft flights. It lets you know when it has created at least one.

- The app only keeps flights that match the aircraft and the time window in question. Otherwise, it creates nothing.
- It discards duplicates and never overwrites an existing flight.
- If there's no network, it tries again later, for up to 7 days.
- Like any draft, a rebuilt flight waits for your approval.

## Comparing a flight with ADS-B

On a flight's screen, tap the ADS-B comparison button.

1. The app compares the flight's four times with those of an ADS-B flight.
2. It only shows fields that are empty or that differ by more than 3 minutes.
3. Apply the ADS-B time field by field with “Use ADS-B”, or all at once with “Use all”.

Nothing changes unless you act. A flight updated this way then shows ADS-B as its source.

## Limitations

- Recovery requires a network.
- The app must be able to identify the aircraft: by its ADS-B code when it's known, for example for an organization aircraft, or from its registration.
- The aircraft must have been picked up by ADS-B receivers. If it wasn't, the app simply says no track was found.
- Departure and arrival aerodromes only appear if the app recognizes them in its aerodrome database. Otherwise, fill them in during review.

## Data sources

ADS-B data comes from adsb.lol and adsbdb, under the ODbL license. Their attributions are in the Settings tab, in the legal and credits section. See [Settings and feedback](/en/docs/app/settings-and-feedback).
