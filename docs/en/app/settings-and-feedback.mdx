The Settings tab (last tab, gear icon) brings together your profile, your preferences, your account and a way to send feedback to the PilotDuty team.

## The sections

- **Profile.** Name, license, ratings, home time zone, acclimatization preference and active regulation. Its logbook section gives access to initial hours, logbook import (PilotDuty+) and the airline roster (Pro). See below.
- **General.** Time display, tracking and permissions. See below.
- **Rest zone and work zone.** With PilotDuty Pro and duty time turned on.
- **Flight following.** By text message and in PilotDuty Manager. See [Flight following](/en/docs/app/flight-following).
- **Account and organizations.** See below.
- **Subscription.** See [Subscriptions](/en/docs/app/subscriptions).
- **Feedback.** See below.
- **Legal and credits.**

The “Duty (Beta)” setting turns on duty time, with PilotDuty Pro. See [Duty time](/en/docs/app/duty-time). The “i” button opens a help video.

Your aircraft are no longer in Settings: they have their own tab, the Aircraft tab (airplane icon). See [iBeacon](/en/docs/app/ibeacon).

## Setting up your pilot profile and initial hours

The pilot profile holds your license information and your ratings. You can also enter your initial flight hours there, so your logbook starts from your current totals.

### Step 1 – Open the Settings tab

From the dashboard, tap the Settings icon (gear) at the bottom right of the screen.

![Dashboard: a red arrow points to the Settings tab at the bottom right](/docs-media/app/settings-and-feedback-settings-tab.png)

### Step 2 – Tap “Pilot Profile”

In the Settings list, choose “Pilot Profile”.

![Settings list: a red arrow points to “Pilot Profile” at the top of the list](/docs-media/app/settings-and-feedback-pilot-profile-menu.png)

### Step 3 – Enter your information

Enter:

- your full name;
- your pilot license number (for example CA123456).

Then turn on the switches that match your ratings:

- airplane license (“Airplane license”) or helicopter license (“Helicopter license”);
- professional license (“Professional license”);
- IFR (“IFR Rated”), multi-engine (“Multi Engine Rated”), seaplane (“Float Rated”), instructor (“Instructor Rated”) and military pilot (“Military Pilot”), as applicable.

This helps the app adapt its features to the way you fly. For example, some choices on a flight's screen depend on your licenses or your instructor rating.

The profile also holds your home time zone, your acclimatization preference and the active regulation. See [Duty time](/en/docs/app/duty-time).

![“Pilot Profile” screen: name John Dow, license CA123456, license and rating switches, and the “Initial flight time” button](/docs-media/app/settings-and-feedback-pilot-profile.png)

### Step 4 – Add your initial flight time

Tap the “Initial flight time” button at the bottom of the profile screen.

A summary screen lets you enter:

- your total flight time (“Total”);
- your pilot in command (“PIC”), co-pilot (“Co-Pilot”) and student (“Student”) hours;
- your total number of cycles (“Total Cycles”);
- your hours by aircraft category (for example “Tricycle Train”, “SkiPlane”, “Tail Wheel”, and so on).

You can also break down your IFR and night hours, by engine, propeller and flight type, and your military hours.

When you're done, tap “Close” to save your initial data.

> This information helps PilotDuty work out accurate totals. It is added to your logbook total. No subscription is required.

![Initial flight time screen: 1,500 hours total, 1,200 cycles, PIC, co-pilot and student hours, hours by category and the “Close” button](/docs-media/app/settings-and-feedback-initial-flight-time.png)

Your profile is now set up and your starting hours are saved. You can come back to “Pilot Profile” at any time to update your ratings or your hours.

To bring in flights from another app or a paper logbook, see [Importing an existing logbook](/en/docs/app/logbook-import). For how initial hours count in your totals, see [Logbook](/en/docs/app/logbook).

## General settings

- **Local time or UTC (“Use local time”).** Choose how times are displayed in the app.
- **Tracking (“GPS Tracking”).** Turns on the location tracking that automatic recording depends on. When it's off, the app doesn't record your flights. You can turn it off when you aren't flying, for example on vacation, to save battery. Remember to turn it back on.
- **Parking time limit (“Parking time limit”).** How long the app waits, in seconds, once the aircraft is parked, before closing the flight. You set it under “Flight overlap threshold”. See [Automatic recording](/en/docs/app/automatic-recording).
- **Permission status (“Authorization Status”).** Shows whether each permission is granted. A declined permission is changed in iOS Settings: the “Open app settings” button takes you there. See [Getting started](/en/docs/app/getting-started).

## Account and organizations

This section shows your account and lets you sign in or out. It opens PilotDuty Manager, lists your organizations and shows the status of flight following in Manager.

## Sending feedback

1. Open the feedback section.
2. Choose the type: bug or feature idea.
3. Enter a title, 5 to 120 characters long.
4. Describe the problem or idea in 10 to 5,000 characters.
5. Send it.

The app then shows your request number, for example PIL-142. Note it if you want to follow up with the team.

- Anyone can report a bug.
- A feature idea requires a PilotDuty+ or PilotDuty Pro subscription, or membership in an organization that subscribes to PilotDuty Manager.
- You must be signed in to your account.
- Technical logs from the app are attached automatically to help the team understand the problem. A notice tells you so before you send.

When an error occurs during a logbook import, the app also offers a link to a prefilled feedback report.

## ADS-B data attributions

The legal and credits section states where the data used by ADS-B recovery comes from: adsb.lol and adsbdb, under the ODbL license. See [ADS-B recovery](/en/docs/app/ads-b-recovery).

## Required update

If the app shows a required-update screen, install the latest version from the App Store. Another screen may say that your data is being upgraded: wait for it to finish.
