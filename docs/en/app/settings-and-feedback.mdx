The Settings tab (last tab, gear icon) brings together your profile, your preferences, your account and a way to send feedback to the PilotDuty team.

## The sections

- **Profile.** Name, license, ratings, home time zone, acclimatization preference and active regulation. Its logbook section gives access to initial hours, logbook import (PilotDuty+) and the airline roster (Pro).
- **General.** Time display, tracking and permissions. See below.
- **Rest zone and work zone.** With PilotDuty Pro and duty time turned on.
- **Flight following.** By text message and in PilotDuty Manager. See [Flight following](/en/docs/app/flight-following).
- **Account and organizations.** See below.
- **Subscription.** See [Subscriptions](/en/docs/app/subscriptions).
- **Feedback.** See below.
- **Legal and credits.**

The “Duty (Beta)” setting turns on duty time, with PilotDuty Pro. See [Duty time](/en/docs/app/duty-time). The “i” button opens a help video.

## General settings

- **Local time or UTC.** Choose how times are displayed in the app.
- **Tracking.** Turns on the location tracking that automatic recording depends on. When it's off, the app doesn't record your flights. You can turn it off when you aren't flying, for example on vacation. Remember to turn it back on.
- **Parking delay.** How long the app waits, in seconds, once the aircraft is parked, before closing the flight. See [Automatic recording](/en/docs/app/automatic-recording).
- **Permission status.** Shows whether each permission is granted. A declined permission is changed in iOS Settings.

## Account and organizations

This section shows your account and lets you sign in or out. It opens PilotDuty Manager, lists your organizations and shows the status of flight following in Manager.

## Sending feedback

1. Open the feedback section.
2. Choose the type: bug or feature idea.
3. Enter a title, 5 to 120 characters long.
4. Describe the problem or idea in 10 to 5,000 characters.
5. Send it.

The app then shows your request number, for example PIL-142. Note it if you want to follow up with the team.

- Anyone can report a bug.
- A feature idea requires a PilotDuty+ or PilotDuty Pro subscription, or membership in an organization that subscribes to PilotDuty Manager.
- You must be signed in to your account.
- Technical logs from the app are attached automatically to help the team understand the problem. A notice tells you so before you send.

When an error occurs during a logbook import, the app also offers a link to a prefilled feedback report.

## ADS-B data attributions

The legal and credits section states where the data used by ADS-B recovery comes from: adsb.lol and adsbdb, under the ODbL license. See [ADS-B recovery](/en/docs/app/ads-b-recovery).

## Required update

If the app shows a required-update screen, install the latest version from the App Store. Another screen may say that your data is being upgraded: wait for it to finish.
