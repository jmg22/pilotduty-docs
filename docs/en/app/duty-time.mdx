Duty time tracks your work, flight and rest periods and compares them with the limits of a regulation.

With PilotDuty Pro. This feature is in beta.

> PilotDuty is a support tool. It replaces neither the regulations nor your operator's rules. Always check your limits against the official texts.

## Turning on duty time

In the Settings tab, turn on “Duty (Beta)”. This setting isn't available without PilotDuty Pro.

Once it's on:

- the Duty tab (timer icon) shows your gauges;
- the dashboard offers a selector between flights and events;
- the “+” button lets you add an event;
- the rest zone and work zone settings become available.

## Events

An event is a period of work, flight or rest. It has a start, an end, and a time zone at the start and at the end. An acclimatization option is available.

For a flight event, enter the number of flights, the total time and the aircraft. The app splits the time across the flights.

- Events wait for your approval on the dashboard, just like flights. Only approved events count toward the gauges.
- On the screen of an event in progress, you can stop, pause or resume it. You can also add a flight to it: by hand, by ADS-B or by scan.
- To correct an approved event, open the day's details in the Duty tab and unapprove the event. The app asks for a reason and adds it to the remark, with the date.
- For now, the time zones offered are those of the Americas.

## Rest and work zones

In the Settings tab, set up a rest zone and a work zone. When you enter or leave one, the app opens or closes the matching event on its own. An event that is too short is deleted automatically.

## Choosing the regulation

You choose the active regulation in the Settings tab, under Profile. Three templates are built in:

- CAR (Canada);
- FAR Part 135 (United States);
- EASA ORO.FTL (Europe).

Built-in templates are locked. To adapt one, create a custom template:

1. Tap “+” in the template list.
2. Start from a built-in template (CAR, FAR or EASA) or from a blank one.
3. Name it. The new template becomes active.

The editor lets you set limits (window, hours, rest days), rest rules, acclimatization, compliance badges and the flight time table. Badge messages are written in English and French. Subparts can't be edited yet.

The active template is saved with your account. If you delete the active template, the app goes back to the CAR template.

## Gauges and badges

The Duty tab shows gauges for each rolling window: 24 hours, 7 days, 28 or 30 days, 90 days and 365 days. Curves compare your duty time and flight time with the limits.

- Badges mark required rest days.
- Tap a day to see its events.
- Info bubbles explain acclimatization, rest and the legend.

Templates and calculations stay in the app: PilotDuty Manager doesn't use them.
