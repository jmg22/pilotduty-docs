This guide walks a new member of an organization through setting up the PilotDuty app on the iPhone, then checking their record in PilotDuty Manager. Follow the two parts in order.

# 1. The PilotDuty app

Procedures and instructions for the iPhone app.

## 1.1 Update your pilot profile

Set up your profile, your ratings and your initial flight time.

### Step 1: Open Settings

From the PilotDuty dashboard, tap the gear icon at the bottom of the screen.

![The app dashboard, with a circle on the gear icon at the bottom right](/docs-media/en/app/new-member-guide/profile-open-settings.png)

### Step 2: Open your pilot profile

Under Settings, select “Pilot Profile”.

![The app settings, with a circle on “Pilot Profile”](/docs-media/en/app/new-member-guide/profile-pilot-profile.png)

### Step 3: Review your pilot profile

Update your name, pilot information, licences and ratings, applicable rules, and home time zone.

![The “Pilot Profile” screen: pilot information (blurred), “700 CARs” rules, time zone and licences](/docs-media/en/app/new-member-guide/profile-review.png)

### Step 4: Hours and imports

Enter your initial flight time (“Initial flight time”). With PilotDuty+, import an electronic logbook or scan a paper logbook (“Import Logbook”). With PilotDuty Pro, subscribe to your airline's iCal feed (“Airline calendar sync”, in beta).

![The “Logbook” section of the profile: “Initial flight time”, “Import Logbook” and “Airline calendar sync”](/docs-media/en/app/new-member-guide/profile-hours-imports.png)

See [Importing an existing logbook](/en/docs/app/logbook-import) and [Airline roster](/en/docs/app/airline-roster).

## 1.2 Add your organization's aircraft

Add your organization's aircraft to your profile.

### Step 1: Open the Aircraft tab

From the dashboard, tap the airplane icon in the bottom bar.

![The dashboard, with a circle on the airplane icon in the bottom bar](/docs-media/en/app/new-member-guide/aircraft-tab.png)

### Step 2: Tap the + button

On the “Tracked Aircraft” screen, tap + in the upper-right corner.

![The “Tracked Aircraft” list, with a circle on the + button in the upper-right corner](/docs-media/en/app/new-member-guide/aircraft-add-button.png)

### Step 3: Select organization aircraft

In the “Add an Aircraft” menu, select “Organization Aircraft”.

![The “Add an Aircraft” menu, with a circle on “Organization Aircraft”](/docs-media/en/app/new-member-guide/aircraft-organization-menu.png)

### Step 4: Select an aircraft

In your organization's aircraft list, tap the aircraft you want to add.

![The “Organization Aircraft” list (organization name blurred), with a circle on aircraft C-GBKQ](/docs-media/en/app/new-member-guide/aircraft-select.png)

### Step 5: Review the aircraft details

Check the registration, the aircraft model and the other information shown.

![The aircraft's “Edit Area” screen: beacon, serial number, registration C-GBKQ, model C-172 and ICAO code](/docs-media/en/app/new-member-guide/aircraft-review-details.png)

### Step 6: Confirm the aircraft was added

Tap the back arrow in the upper-left corner. Make sure the aircraft appears in “Tracked Aircraft”. To add another aircraft, repeat steps 1 to 6.

![The “Tracked Aircraft” list, with a circle on the newly added aircraft C-GBKQ at the bottom](/docs-media/en/app/new-member-guide/aircraft-tracked-list.png)

See [With your organization](/en/docs/app/with-your-organization).

## 1.3 Set up automatic flight tracking

Check permissions and turn on flight tracking.

### Step 1: Open Settings

From the dashboard, tap the gear icon in the bottom bar.

![The dashboard, with a circle on the gear icon at the bottom right](/docs-media/en/app/new-member-guide/tracking-open-settings.png)

### Step 2: Select General

In Settings, select “General”.

![The app settings, with a circle on “General”](/docs-media/en/app/new-member-guide/tracking-general.png)

### Step 3: Check permissions

Under “Autorisation Status”, confirm that “Location” is set to “Always” and that notifications, “Motion” and “Camera” are granted (green). Resolve any yellow or red status in the iPhone settings.

![The “Autorisation Status” screen: “Location” set to “Always”, with “Notification”, “Motion” and “Camera” “Granted”](/docs-media/en/app/new-member-guide/tracking-permissions.png)

### Step 4: Set the flight separation delay

In “General”, turn on “GPS Tracking” and check the “Parking time limit”, under “Flight overlap threshold”. The screenshot shows it at 120 seconds. Increase it if your airport has longer ground delays.

![The “General” settings: “GPS Tracking” on and “Parking time limit” at 120](/docs-media/en/app/new-member-guide/tracking-parking-time-limit.png)

See [Setting the parking time limit](/en/docs/app/automatic-recording#setting-the-parking-time-limit).

### Step 5: Open Flight Following

Return to Settings, then select “Flight Following”.

![The app settings, with a circle on “Flight Following”](/docs-media/en/app/new-member-guide/tracking-flight-following.png)

### Step 6: Turn on tracking in PilotDuty Manager

Under “Flight Following”, turn on “Enable flight Following on PilotDuty Manager”. Your organization can then follow your flights in PilotDuty Manager. Without a subscribed organization, this setting is turned off.

![The “Flight Following” settings, with a circle on the “Enable flight Following on PilotDuty Manager” switch](/docs-media/en/app/new-member-guide/tracking-enable-manager.png)

See [Flight following](/en/docs/app/flight-following).

## 1.4 Automatic flight recording

From aircraft detection to flight approval, including editing and deleting.

### Step 1: Aircraft detection

Near the aircraft, PilotDuty automatically detects a flight event and displays its status.

![The dashboard with the “Flight Event Started” banner](/docs-media/en/app/new-member-guide/recording-detection.png)

### Step 2: Flight status

The top banner shows the flight phase: event started (“Flight Event Started”), taxiing (“Taxiing”), flying (“Flying”) or parked (“Parked”).

![The dashboard with the “Taxiing” banner](/docs-media/en/app/new-member-guide/recording-flight-status.png)

### Step 3: In-flight recording

At takeoff, a flight card appears with times and a progress bar.

![The dashboard with the “Flying” banner and the card of the flight in progress](/docs-media/en/app/new-member-guide/recording-in-flight.png)

### Step 4: Landing notification

After landing, if flight following is turned on, tap the “Confirm safe” notification or reopen the app to confirm your safe arrival.

![The iPhone lock screen with the “Confirm safe” notification](/docs-media/en/app/new-member-guide/recording-landing-notification.png)

### Step 5: Confirm safe arrival

In the confirmation prompt, tap “Yes” to confirm you landed safely.

![The “Confirmation” prompt: “Do you want to confirm safe landing?”, with “No” and “Yes”](/docs-media/en/app/new-member-guide/recording-confirm-safe.png)

### Step 6: Review flight data

Review the recorded flight card and check the times, aircraft and airports before approval.

![The recorded flight card on the dashboard: times, CSY4 airports and date](/docs-media/en/app/new-member-guide/recording-review.png)

### Step 7: Edit a flight

Tap the flight card to open its details (“Edit Flight”) and correct information as needed.

![The “Edit Flight” screen: aircraft, departure airport and times](/docs-media/en/app/new-member-guide/recording-edit.png)

### Step 8: Approve the flight

On the dashboard, swipe the flight card right and tap “Approve”.

![The card swiped right, with the blue “Approve” button](/docs-media/en/app/new-member-guide/recording-approve.png)

### Step 9: View the logbook

Open the “Logbook” tab to see today's flights or earlier entries.

![The “Logbook” tab: total flight time and today's flight](/docs-media/en/app/new-member-guide/recording-logbook.png)

### Step 10: Unapprove a flight

In the logbook, swipe the flight card left and tap “UnApprove”. Then edit the flight on the dashboard and approve it again.

![In “Logbook”, the card swiped left, with the yellow “UnApprove” button](/docs-media/en/app/new-member-guide/recording-unapprove.png)

### Step 11: Delete a flight

To remove an incorrect entry, swipe the flight card left on the dashboard and tap “Delete”.

![The card swiped left, with the red “Delete” button](/docs-media/en/app/new-member-guide/recording-delete.png)

See [Reviewing and approving flights](/en/docs/app/review-and-approval).

# 2. PilotDuty Manager

Procedures and instructions for [PilotDuty Manager](https://manager.pilotduty.com), on a computer or in your phone's browser.

## 2.1 Review your personal information

Organization, pilot record, client record and settings.

### Step 1: Open your organizations

From the main menu, tap “Your Organizations”.

![The Manager main menu, with a circle on “Your Organizations”](/docs-media/en/app/new-member-guide/manager-open-organizations.png)

### Step 2: Select your organization

Select the right organization to review the information associated with it.

![The “Your Organizations” window: “Select an organization below to switch to it” (organization name blurred)](/docs-media/en/app/new-member-guide/manager-select-organization.png)

### Step 3: Open your pilot record

Return to the main menu and select “Pilots” to open your pilot record.

![The Manager main menu, with a circle on “Pilots”](/docs-media/en/app/new-member-guide/manager-open-pilots.png)

### Step 4: Review your pilot information

Go through each tab and dropdown to update your pilot details, licences, ratings and medical certificate.

![The pilot record (name and contact details blurred): hours, “Overview”, “Duty”, “Flights” and “Bookings” tabs, licences and competencies](/docs-media/en/app/new-member-guide/manager-pilot-record.png)

### Step 5: Open your client record

If you are also a client of the organization, return to the main menu and select “Clients”.

![The Manager main menu, with a circle on “Clients”](/docs-media/en/app/new-member-guide/manager-open-clients.png)

### Step 6: Review your client information

Check your contact information, billing address and other client details, for accurate invoicing.

![The client record (name and contact details blurred): bookings, costs, payments and prepaid balance](/docs-media/en/app/new-member-guide/manager-client-record.png)

### Step 7: Open Settings

Return to the main menu and select “Settings”.

![The Manager main menu, with a circle on “Settings”](/docs-media/en/app/new-member-guide/manager-open-settings.png)

### Step 8: Review your details

In the “Profile” tab, under “My Details”, review and update your name, email address, phone number, emergency contact and other personal information.

![The “My Details” section of the “Profile” tab: picture, name, email, phone and communication language (details blurred)](/docs-media/en/app/new-member-guide/manager-my-details.png)

## 2.2 Add a flight manually

For members who don't have the PilotDuty app on their phone.

### Step 1: Select Pilots

From the main menu, select “Pilots” to open your pilot record.

![The Manager main menu, with a circle on “Pilots”](/docs-media/en/app/new-member-guide/manual-flight-menu.png)

### Step 2: Open the Flights tab

In your pilot record, select the “Flights” tab.

![The pilot record, with a circle on the “Flights” tab](/docs-media/en/app/new-member-guide/manual-flight-flights-tab.png)

### Step 3: Show the flight list

In the dropdown, choose “Flight list” to see recorded flights.

![The “Flights” tab of the pilot record, with its dropdown](/docs-media/en/app/new-member-guide/manual-flight-list.png)

### Step 4: Tap Add Flight

In the flight list, tap “+ Add Flight”.

![The flight list, with a circle on the “+ Add Flight” button](/docs-media/en/app/new-member-guide/manual-flight-add-button.png)

### Step 5: Enter flight details

Enter the aircraft, airports, times and all required details. All four times are required. Review, then tap “Add”.

![The “Add Flight” window: aircraft, call sign, type, airports and times](/docs-media/en/app/new-member-guide/manual-flight-form.png)

### Step 6: Check the flight sheet

Return to “Flightsheet” and check that the flight is linked to the correct booking, if a booking was made.

![The Manager main menu, with a circle on “Flightsheet”](/docs-media/en/app/new-member-guide/manual-flight-flightsheet.png)

See [Pilots and duty time](/en/docs/manager/pilots-and-duty-time).

## 2.3 Set up the organization

For an organization you create or manage. Changing the organization's settings requires the Chief Instructor role or higher.

### Step 1: Open your organizations

From the main menu, tap “Your Organizations”.

![The Manager main menu](/docs-media/en/app/new-member-guide/organization-open-organizations.png)

### Step 2: Create an organization

In the organization list, tap “+ New organization”, then complete the required information.

![The “Your Organizations” list (names blurred) and the “New organization” button](/docs-media/en/app/new-member-guide/organization-new.png)

See [Getting started with PilotDuty Manager](/en/docs/manager/getting-started).

### Step 3: Open Aircrafts

From the main menu, select “Aircrafts” to view your organization's aircraft.

![The Manager main menu, with “Aircrafts”](/docs-media/en/app/new-member-guide/organization-aircraft-menu.png)

### Step 4: Add or edit an aircraft

Tap “+ New aircraft” to add an aircraft. Use the three-dot menu beside an aircraft to edit its profile.

![The “Aircrafts” list: “New aircraft” button, types, call signs and next inspections](/docs-media/en/app/new-member-guide/organization-aircraft-list.png)

See [Fleet and maintenance](/en/docs/manager/fleet-and-maintenance).

### Step 5: Open Settings

From the main menu, tap “Settings”, then select the “Organization” tab.

![The “Organization” tab of the settings: organization logo, name, address, phone and email (blurred)](/docs-media/en/app/new-member-guide/organization-settings.png)

### Step 6: Set up the organization

In the dropdown, review the sections from top to bottom: general information, members and SMS flight following.

![The “Organization” tab dropdown: “General”, “Members”, “Workshops”, “PTR Templates”, “E-Signature Policy”, “Flight Following SMS” and “Pending Invites”](/docs-media/en/app/new-member-guide/organization-settings-sections.png)

### Step 7: Set up bookings

Under “Settings”, open the “Booking” tab. Complete each section in order to set up bookings through invoicing.

![The “Booking” tab of the settings: “Organization”, “Aircraft”, “Pilot/Instructor”, “Classrooms”, “Activity resources”, “Service”, “Notifications” and “Payment” sections](/docs-media/en/app/new-member-guide/organization-booking-settings.png)
