Flight following lets someone know about your takeoffs and arrivals. There are two separate kinds: by text message, to a person you choose, and in PilotDuty Manager, for your organization.

Both are set in the Settings tab, on the flight following row. A dot on that row shows your coverage status.

## Text message following

With PilotDuty+ or PilotDuty Pro.

Your follower receives text messages at key moments of the flight. They don't need the app.

### Setting up your follower

1. Enter your follower's name and phone number, in international format.
2. Ask for their consent, then confirm in the app that you have it. This confirmation is required for each new number. Nothing is checked in advance.
3. Choose which messages to send: takeoff, landing, delay.
4. Turn following on. The app asks for permission to send notifications.

Once you confirm consent, your follower receives a welcome message.

### The messages

Depending on your choices, your follower can receive:

- a message at takeoff;
- a message at landing;
- an arrival confirmation;
- a delay alert if your arrival isn't confirmed.

The PilotDuty server writes and sends the messages, not your phone. Your iPhone therefore needs a network to trigger them.

### If your follower replies STOP

A follower who replies STOP no longer receives messages. You can't turn following back on for that number until they reply START.

### The coverage dot

- Red: nobody will receive your messages.
- Orange: coverage isn't verified.

A dashboard banner may also flag a coverage problem. Tap it to open the flight following settings.

## Confirming your arrival as safe

After landing, a followed flight waits for your arrival confirmation on the dashboard. Swipe it to the left and tap “Safe”. You can also confirm it from the event's screen by swiping the flight to the left.

A flight linked to flight following can't be deleted from its event.

## Following in PilotDuty Manager

For members of an organization that subscribes to PilotDuty Manager.

Turn on the PilotDuty Manager flight following setting. Without a subscribed organization, this setting is turned off.

- At takeoff, the app creates the flight's tracking record and adds milestones to it: position, altitude and heading.
- Your organization follows the flight in Manager: flight sheet, map and delay alerts.
- The tracking is visible in Manager even before you approve the flight.
- The flight is marked as arrived when you confirm it as safe. At your next takeoff, the app also confirms the previous flight.

Manager following doesn't depend on text message following. You can turn on either one, or both.

## Good to know

Following starts at the detected takeoff. If automatic recording isn't working, following won't either. See [Automatic recording](/en/docs/app/automatic-recording).
