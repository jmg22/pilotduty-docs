The pilot health screen (“Pilot Status”) brings your aviation documents together: medical certificates, licenses and competencies. To let PilotDuty calculate the validity of your medical, license and proficiency checks, enter your personal data. The screen then shows, for each document, the days left before it expires.

With PilotDuty Pro.

The screenshots on this page date from 2025. Since then, you can record several documents of each type, each with its own authority, so the current screen may look slightly different.

## Step 1: Open the pilot health screen

1. Open the dashboard.
2. Tap the validity badge (the shield) in the top-left corner.
3. The “Pilot Status” screen opens.

The badge shows the days left on the document closest to expiring, in the color of its status.

- Without PilotDuty Pro, the button is locked and presents the subscription.
- If memos from your organization need your attention, the button shows an envelope instead. See [With your organization](/en/docs/app/with-your-organization).

## Step 2: Enter your medical certificate

1. Tap “+”, choose a medical, then the authority: Transport Canada, FAA, EASA or another authority.
2. Choose the category or class offered by the authority: category 1 or 3, class 1 to 3, or LAPL.
3. Enter your date of birth and the date of your last medical exam (“Last Medical Date”). The app calculates validity using the chosen authority's rules. For another authority, enter the expiry date yourself.
4. Optional: add your doctor's name, phone number and address. Once this information is filled in, a call button and a navigation button appear: you can call your doctor, or open their address in Maps, from the record.
5. Optional: add a picture of your medical certificate.

The shorter EASA class 1 validity after age 60 isn't taken into account. Check that date yourself.

![The medical record: date of birth, last medical date, doctor's details with the call and navigation buttons, and certificate photo (blurred)](/docs-media/en/app/pilot-health/pilot-health-medical.png)

## Step 3: Enter your license

1. Tap “+”, choose a license, then the authority.
2. Tap the date box and select your license dates, including its expiry date.
3. Optional: add a photo of your license.

A license record also includes:

- the type: license or student pilot permit;
- the code and number;
- the airplane and helicopter categories;
- the class rating;
- endorsements, which you turn on one by one;
- notes.

![The license record: license expiry date and document photo (blurred)](/docs-media/en/app/pilot-health/pilot-health-license.png)

## Step 4: Enter your proficiency checks

Competencies cover recurring checks and qualifications: PPC, IPC, PCC, check ride, rating, endorsement, language proficiency (ELP), course, recurrent training, or a custom competency.

1. Tap “+”, choose a competency, then the authority.
2. Enter, for example for a proficiency check:
   - the validity start date;
   - the aircraft type, for example C172;
   - the check type, for example PCC or PPC;
   - the validity period, in months, for example 12. It is preset according to the chosen code;
   - optional: the examiner's name and remarks.

The fields adapt to the type you choose: aircraft, rating or endorsement code, related license, language proficiency level (4, 5 or 6), examiner, result, subject, remarks and photo.

![A proficiency check record: validity start date, C172 aircraft type, check type, 12-month validity period, examiner and remarks](/docs-media/en/app/pilot-health/pilot-health-proficiency-check.png)

## Editing or deleting a document

Every change is saved right away: there is no save button. To delete a document, swipe it to the left.

## Days left and colors

Once the information is recorded, PilotDuty automatically calculates the number of days left before each document expires.

- Green: the document is valid.
- Yellow: the expiry date is getting close. For a medical, yellow shows 30 days before; for a license, 90 days before.
- Red: the date has passed.

The dashboard shield shows the least favorable status across all your documents, in green, yellow or red. Open the pilot health screen regularly to keep an eye on your dates.

## Records issued by your organization

If your organization issued you a license, an endorsement or a competency in PilotDuty Manager, the record appears in the app as read-only. You can't edit or delete it.

Your records are the same in the app and in Manager: if you belong to an organization, its staff can see them on your pilot profile.
