The pilot health screen brings your aviation documents together: medical certificates, licenses and competencies. For each one, it shows the days left before it expires.

With PilotDuty Pro.

## Opening the screen

Tap the shield at the top left of the dashboard. It shows the days left on the document closest to expiring, in the color of its status.

- Without PilotDuty Pro, the button is locked and presents the subscription.
- If memos from your organization need your attention, the button shows an envelope instead. See [With your organization](/en/docs/app/with-your-organization).

## Adding a document

1. Tap “+”.
2. Choose a medical, a license or a competency.
3. Choose the authority: Transport Canada, FAA, EASA or another authority.
4. Fill in the record. Every change is saved right away.

To delete a document, swipe it to the right.

## Medical certificate

- Choose the category or class offered by the authority: category 1 or 3, class 1 to 3, or LAPL.
- Enter your date of birth and the date of your last exam. The app calculates validity using the chosen authority's rules.
- For another authority, enter the expiry date yourself.
- Add your doctor's contact details. You can then call them, or open their address in Maps, from the record.
- Attach a photo of the certificate if you like.

The shorter EASA class 1 validity after age 60 isn't taken into account. Check that date yourself.

## Licenses

A license record includes:

- the type: license or student pilot permit;
- the code and number;
- the dates;
- the airplane and helicopter categories;
- the class rating;
- endorsements, which you turn on one by one;
- notes and a photo.

## Competencies

Competencies cover recurring checks and qualifications: PPC, IPC, PCC, check ride, rating, endorsement, language proficiency (ELP), course, recurrent training, or a custom competency.

The fields adapt to the type you choose: aircraft, rating or endorsement code, related license, language proficiency level (4, 5 or 6), examiner, result, subject, remarks and photo. Validity is preset according to the chosen code.

## Days left and colors

- Green: the document is valid.
- Yellow: the expiry date is getting close. For a medical, yellow shows 30 days before; for a license, 90 days before.
- Red: the date has passed.

The dashboard shield shows the least favorable status across all your documents. Open the pilot health screen regularly to keep an eye on your dates.

## Records issued by your organization

If your organization issued you a license, an endorsement or a competency in PilotDuty Manager, the record appears in the app as read-only. You can't edit or delete it.

Your records are the same in the app and in Manager: if you belong to an organization, its staff can see them on your pilot profile.
