Every detected flight and event waits for your approval before it goes into your logbook. You review it, correct it if needed, then approve it.

## Before you approve: what to know

All events listed on the Dashboard are awaiting approval. They must be approved to appear in your logbook and in your duty time.

If you belong to one or more organizations that use PilotDuty Manager, your events show up there once approved: the app and Manager share the same database.

**Once approved:**

- the event is locked: you can no longer edit or delete it;
- to change it, first unapprove it, edit it, then approve it again. See “Unapproving a flight” below.

**With PilotDuty Manager:**

- if an event is edited in PilotDuty Manager, the app receives the change;
- if an event is deleted in PilotDuty Manager, the app unapproves it, tells you why and puts it back on the Dashboard, with the items awaiting approval.

Delete it if the deletion was intended, or approve it again if it was a mistake.

> This workflow preserves the integrity and traceability of your data.

> **Pilot responsibility.** The app records, but you remain responsible for what goes into your logbook. It is up to you to check the accuracy of all automatically recorded data by reviewing and approving each event.

## The dashboard

The Dashboard tab (first tab, speedometer icon) is your approval queue. Items you haven't reviewed yet appear under “Awaiting Approval”, in chronological order. The tab icon shows a badge with the number of items to handle.

![“Awaiting Approval” dashboard with a “Flight Event” on March 28, from 17:06 to 21:38, lasting 4.5 hours](/docs-media/en/app/review-and-approval/review-and-approval-awaiting-approval.png)

If duty time is turned on, a selector lets you switch between flights and duty events. See [Duty time](/en/docs/app/duty-time).

A flight that is still recording can't be approved or deleted.

## Approving a flight or an event

1. Swipe the flight or event to the right.
2. Tap “Approve”. The app asks you to confirm.

![Event swiped to the right, with the blue “Approve” button on the left](/docs-media/en/app/review-and-approval/review-and-approval-approve-event.png)

For a followed flight waiting for your arrival confirmation, the right swipe shows “Safe” instead. See [Flight following](/en/docs/app/flight-following).

You can approve without a network. The flight is sent once the connection is back.

## Deleting a flight or an event

1. Swipe the flight or event to the left.
2. Tap “Delete”.

![Event swiped to the left, with the red “Delete” button on the right](/docs-media/en/app/review-and-approval/review-and-approval-delete-event.png)

This is how you delete a flight detected by mistake.

## Editing an event

Tap an event to open it and see its details. A flight event's screen lets you edit:

- the start date, time and time zone (“Event Start Date”, “Start Time Zone”);
- the stop date, time and time zone (“Event Stop Date”, “Stop Time Zone”);
- the acclimatization option (“Time Zone Acclimatization Option”): “Step Acclimatization” or “1h/day per TZ”;
- the remarks (“Remarks”).

![Event screen: start and stop dates and time zones, acclimatization option, remarks and the start of the flight list](/docs-media/en/app/review-and-approval/review-and-approval-edit-event.png)

While the event is recording, its fields are locked. On the screen of an event in progress, you can stop, pause or resume it.

### The flight list

For a flight event, the “Flights” section shows one card per flight (one per leg). The “Add Flight” button adds a flight to the event: by hand, from ADS-B (PilotDuty Pro) or by scan. See [Manual entry and ACARS scan](/en/docs/app/manual-entry-and-acars-scan).

![Event screen with a single flight in the “Flights” section, from CYBG to CYQB, and the “Add Flight” button](/docs-media/en/app/review-and-approval/review-and-approval-event-flights.png)

### Deleting a flight from the event

Swipe the flight to the left, then tap “Delete”.

![Flight swiped to the left in the event's list, with the red “Delete” button](/docs-media/en/app/review-and-approval/review-and-approval-delete-flight.png)

A flight in progress, or one linked to flight following, can't be deleted from its event.

### Editing a flight in the event

Tap a flight to edit it. See “Editing a flight” below.

## The confidence score

For a flight detected by GPS, the app scores the reliability of each of the four times and of both aerodromes, plus an overall score.

- Green: reliable.
- Yellow: worth checking.
- Red: unreliable, review this value.

Tap a score ring to read what it means. A capsule also shows the flight's source (GPS, ADS-B, manual entry or import) and its confidence percentage.

When you correct a time or an aerodrome by hand, that field's score disappears: the value is no longer a measurement. The score is a review aid and does not show up in PilotDuty Manager.

## Suggested estimated times

If GPS missed the moment of a step, the app estimates the actual time and suggests it under the field, with its margin of uncertainty. Nothing changes until you tap “Apply”.

## Editing a flight

Tap a flight to open it in the “Edit Flight” screen.

At the top of the screen, you find the aircraft's registration and type (“Aircraft Info”), the departure aerodrome (“Departure Airport”), then the off-block (“Off Block Time”), takeoff (“Take Off Time”) and landing (“Landing Time”) times, and so on.

![“Edit Flight” screen: registration C-FFFF, type C172, departure CYBG, off-block, takeoff and landing times](/docs-media/en/app/review-and-approval/review-and-approval-edit-flight-times.png)

Further down, you find the flight details:

- the flight type (“Flight Type”): IFR, night (“Night”), operating rule (“Operating Rule”), flight type (for example “Cross Country”) and “Single Pilot”;
- the number of takeoffs and landings (“Number of cycles”);
- the aircraft's characteristics: category, engine and propeller.

![Lower part of the “Edit Flight” screen: IFR, “Night”, “Operating Rule”, “Flight Type”, “Single Pilot”, number of cycles, category, engine and propeller](/docs-media/en/app/review-and-approval/review-and-approval-edit-flight-details.png)

In short, the flight screen lets you edit:

- the four times (start of taxi, takeoff, landing, parking);
- the departure and arrival aerodromes;
- the aircraft, flight type and counts;
- the category, engine and propeller;
- the remarks and attachments.

Every change is saved right away; there is no save button. Durations recalculate on their own. Some choices depend on your profile, such as your licenses or your instructor rating.

With PilotDuty Pro, the flight screen also offers an ADS-B comparison. See [ADS-B recovery](/en/docs/app/ads-b-recovery).

## Attachments

You can attach to a flight a photo taken with the camera, an image from your photo library, or a file (image, PDF or text). Tap an attachment to view it full screen. You can also delete it. Adding and deleting attachments requires a network.

In the logbook, a flight's paperclip shows its attachments and remarks.

## Unapproving a flight

Unapproving a flight takes only a few gestures. Do it when a flight or its details need correcting, or when it should be removed from your duty time. Unapproving one flight unapproves its whole event, with all the other flights in that event.

### Step 1 – Open the logbook

Tap the Logbook tab in the bottom bar.

### Step 2 – Find the flight

Find the flight you want to unapprove in the list. Swipe it to the left: a yellow “UnApprove” button appears on the right of the flight.

### Step 3 – Tap “UnApprove”

Tap “UnApprove”. A confirmation screen asks you for the reason.

![Logbook with a flight swiped to the left and the yellow “UnApprove” button on the right](/docs-media/en/app/review-and-approval/review-and-approval-unapprove-flight.png)

### Step 4 – Enter the reason

1. Enter the reason in the text box.
2. Tap “Submit Reason” to confirm.

> **Important.** When you unapprove a flight, the event that contains it and all the flights in that event are unapproved. The unapproved event is back on the Dashboard, with the items awaiting approval. Correct it, then approve it again.

![“You are about to disapprove an event, please provide the reason” screen, with the “Reason” box and the “Submit Reason” button](/docs-media/en/app/review-and-approval/review-and-approval-unapprove-reason.png)

> If you unapproved a flight by mistake, approve it again from the Dashboard, with the items awaiting approval.

### Good to know

- Unapproving also works offline; it takes effect when the connection is back.
- A flight that came from your airline roster leaves the logbook and is offered to you again from the roster.
- With duty time, you can also unapprove an event from a day's details in the Duty tab. See [Duty time](/en/docs/app/duty-time).

## Version history

In the logbook, press and hold a flight, then choose to view its version history. The timeline shows each version of the flight, newest first: creation, edits, approvals and unapprovals. For each one, you see the date and time in UTC, the reason, the author and the fields that changed. The app shows up to the 10 most recent versions.
