This page explains what each pilot plan unlocks, Free, PilotDuty+ and PilotDuty Pro, and how to manage your subscription. Prices are listed on the website's Pricing page.

## Free

- Up to 3 flights per month. Beyond that, the dashboard shows the subscription screen instead of your flights to approve.
- Automatic recording by GPS, manual entry and ACARS scan.
- Review, confidence score and suggested estimated times.
- Logbook: list, filters, map, currency and initial hours.
- If you belong to an organization: memos, aircraft profiles and defect reporting.
- Bug reports.

## PilotDuty+

Everything in Free, plus:

- unlimited flights;
- beacon-triggered recording with an iBeacon;
- logbook export to PDF and CSV;
- logbook import: CSV files from LogTen Pro, ForeFlight and MyFlightbook, and paper logbook by photo (beta);
- text message flight following;
- sending feature ideas.

## PilotDuty Pro

Everything in PilotDuty+, plus:

- duty time (beta), with the CAR, FAR and EASA templates, custom templates, and rest and work zones;
- pilot health: medical, licenses and competencies;
- ADS-B recovery;
- airline roster (beta).

A feature that requires a plan shows that plan's badge. Tapping it opens the subscription screen.

## Buying and managing your subscription

Subscriptions are bought in the app, through the App Store, monthly or yearly. You'll find them in the Settings tab, or by tapping a feature that requires a plan.

- Apple handles payment and renewal. To change plans or cancel, go to your Apple ID subscriptions in iOS Settings.
- The app's subscription screens let you restore your purchases, for example on a new iPhone, and redeem a code.
- PilotDuty Manager doesn't sell these plans: they can only be bought in the app.

## Free beacon with an annual subscription

An annual PilotDuty+ or PilotDuty Pro subscription includes a free iBeacon in the first year. After your purchase, the app asks for your shipping address. If you skip that step, a dashboard banner reminds you. See [iBeacon](/en/docs/app/ibeacon).

## Your organization's subscription

An organization's PilotDuty Manager subscription (Growth and Unlimited plans) is separate from your personal subscription. Neither one converts into the other.

- It's paid by the organization, not through the App Store.
- It lifts the limit of 3 flights per month for its members.
- It enables flight following in Manager and sending feature ideas.
- It doesn't give you PilotDuty+ or PilotDuty Pro. For duty time, pilot health or the airline roster, you need a personal subscription.

Likewise, your personal subscription doesn't pay for your organization's subscription. See [With your organization](/en/docs/app/with-your-organization).
