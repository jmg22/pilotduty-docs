If your organization uses PilotDuty Manager, the app becomes your link to it: your flights show up there, you read its memos and you look up its aircraft.

## Becoming a member

Your organization invites you from PilotDuty Manager. In the app, use the same account as in Manager: both share the same database. Your organizations are listed in the Settings tab, in the account section.

## Your flights in Manager

- An approved flight shows up on your profile in Manager.
- A flight you haven't approved stays on your iPhone. Only its flight following can be visible in Manager.
- You can also create or correct your flights in Manager. The app receives those changes.
- If your organization deletes one of your flights in Manager, the app unapproves it, tells you why and puts it back in your approval queue.

## Memos

Your organization's memos arrive in the app. This feature doesn't depend on your personal subscription. The app is for reading memos: you can't write them in it.

- **Where to find them.** When a memo needs your attention, the button at the top left of the dashboard shows an envelope and a counter. The memos row also appears at the top of the pilot health screen, even without PilotDuty Pro.
- **List.** Memos are grouped into two sections: current, and expired or superseded.
- **Reading.** Opening a memo marks it as read.
- **Acknowledging.** If the memo asks for it, tap the button to confirm you have read and understood it. After the deadline, the memo is marked late, but your acknowledgment is still accepted.
- **Superseded memo.** The app tells you and offers to open the new one.
- **Attachments.** Tap an attachment to view it. You need a network.
- **Links.** A link to a memo in Manager, or a memo notification, opens the memo directly in the app.
- **Urgent memo.** A banner stays on the dashboard until you acknowledge it.

The categories are Safety, Operations, Maintenance, Training and Administration.

## Aircraft and maintenance

In the Aircraft tab (airplane icon), each organization aircraft's card shows badges for the next inspection due, in hours, days or cycles, in green, orange or red.

Tap the aircraft to open its profile: photo, registration, type, specifications (weight in pounds, fuel, cruise speed), airframe hours and cycles, upcoming inspections and active defects. This information is read-only. Aircraft and maintenance are managed in Manager.

## Reporting a defect

With the Pilot role, or a higher role, in the organization that owns the aircraft.

1. Open the aircraft's profile.
2. Tap the triangle in the top bar.
3. Describe the defect and choose its severity: “No-Go”, “Go-If” or “Info”. If needed, add the location (ICAO code), the ATA chapter, and the date and time.
4. Send it. You need a network.

The report appears in Manager's maintenance module. You can't attach a photo to it from the app.

## Picking an organization aircraft

During the welcome flow or from the Aircraft tab, pick an aircraft from your organizations' list. The app takes over its setup: type, category, engine, propeller, IFR, single-pilot, takeoff speed, picture, rules and ADS-B code. If the aircraft has a beacon in Manager, beacon detection is turned on automatically.

## Subscribed organization

If one of your organizations subscribes to PilotDuty Manager:

- the free limit of 3 flights per month no longer applies;
- you can turn on flight following in Manager;
- you can send feature ideas.

The organization's subscription doesn't include PilotDuty+ or PilotDuty Pro. See [Subscriptions](/en/docs/app/subscriptions).

The app has no booking features: bookings are made in Manager.
