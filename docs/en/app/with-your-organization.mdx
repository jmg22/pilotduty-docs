If your organization uses PilotDuty Manager, the app becomes your link to it: your flights show up there, you read its memos and you look up its aircraft.

Connecting to an organization lets PilotDuty share your flights and duty events with your team. This is especially useful for operators who want to follow their flights in real time in PilotDuty Manager, or for aircraft owners and groups who want to track flight time for maintenance.

## Connecting to PilotDuty Manager

Your organization invites you from PilotDuty Manager. The invitation is valid for 7 days. In the app, use the same account as in Manager: both share the same database.

### Step 1: Open the account section

Open the Settings tab, at the bottom right, then the account and organizations section.

![The Settings tab in 2025, with an arrow pointing to the “PilotDuty Manager” row](/docs-media/en/app/with-your-organization/with-your-organization-settings-manager.png)

In this 2025 screenshot, the section was called “PilotDuty Manager”. The Settings tab has changed since, but the section plays the same role.

### Step 2: Sign in

If you've completed the welcome flow, you already have a PilotDuty account: it's the same one as in Manager.

If you're not signed in yet, use the same method as during the welcome flow, or as in Manager:

- email and password;
- Sign in with Apple;
- Sign in with Google.

If you don't have an account yet, create one in the app, during the welcome flow, or on [PilotDuty Manager](https://manager.pilotduty.com).

### Step 3: Manage your connection

This section lists the organizations you belong to, for example “Acme Aviation”. It also shows your account, the status of flight following in Manager and the “Sign out” button.

The status of flight following in Manager shows as:

- “Activated” (green badge): flight following in Manager is turned on;
- “Disabled”: it is turned off.

![The account section in 2025: the “Acme Aviation” and “test” organizations, the account, the “Flight Following” “Activated” status and the “Sign out” button](/docs-media/en/app/with-your-organization/with-your-organization-organizations-list.png)

Once you're signed in, your approved flights and events show up in your organizations. See the next section.

### Notes

- Flight following isn't required to share your flights with your organization. Your approved flights show up in Manager even without it. Flight following only shares your tracking during the flight. See [Flight following](/en/docs/app/flight-following).
- You must be signed in for your flights and duty events (work and rest, with PilotDuty Pro) to show up in Manager.
- In Manager, every member sees their own profile. Staff with the Instructor role or higher see your complete profile: logbook, duty time, medical and competencies.
- The app and Manager share the same database: a change made on one side shows on the other.

## Your flights in Manager

- An approved flight or duty event shows up on your profile in Manager. This doesn't depend on flight following, which only shares your tracking during the flight.
- A flight you haven't approved stays on your iPhone. Only its flight following can be visible in Manager.
- You can also create or correct your flights in Manager. The app receives those changes.
- If your organization deletes one of your flights in Manager, the app unapproves it, tells you why and puts it back in your approval queue. Delete it if the deletion was intended, or approve it again if it was a mistake.

## Memos

Your organization's memos arrive in the app. This feature doesn't depend on your personal subscription. The app is for reading memos: you can't write them in it.

- **Where to find them.** When a memo needs your attention, the button at the top left of the dashboard shows an envelope and a counter. The memos row also appears at the top of the pilot health screen, even without PilotDuty Pro.
- **List.** Memos are grouped into two sections: current, and expired or superseded.
- **Reading.** Opening a memo marks it as read.
- **Acknowledging.** If the memo asks for it, tap the button to confirm you have read and understood it. After the deadline, the memo is marked late, but your acknowledgment is still accepted.
- **Superseded memo.** The app tells you and offers to open the new one.
- **Attachments.** Tap an attachment to view it. You need a network.
- **Links.** A link to a memo in Manager, or a memo notification, opens the memo directly in the app.
- **Urgent memo.** A banner stays on the dashboard until you acknowledge it.

The categories are Safety, Operations, Maintenance, Training and Administration.

## Aircraft and maintenance

In the Aircraft tab (airplane icon), each organization aircraft's card shows badges for the next inspection due, in hours, days or cycles, in green, orange or red.

Tap the aircraft to open its profile: photo, registration, type, specifications (weight in pounds, fuel, cruise speed), airframe hours and cycles, upcoming inspections and active defects. This information is read-only. Aircraft and maintenance are managed in Manager.

## Reporting a defect

With the Pilot role, or a higher role, in the organization that owns the aircraft.

1. Open the aircraft's profile.
2. Tap the triangle in the top bar.
3. Describe the defect and choose its severity: “No-Go”, “Go-If” or “Info”. If needed, add the location (ICAO code), the ATA chapter, and the date and time.
4. Send it. You need a network.

The report appears in Manager's maintenance module. You can't attach a photo to it from the app.

## Picking an organization aircraft

During the welcome flow or from the Aircraft tab, pick an aircraft from your organizations' list. The app takes over its setup: type, category, engine, propeller, IFR, single-pilot, takeoff speed, picture, rules and ADS-B code. If the aircraft has a beacon in Manager, beacon detection is turned on automatically.

## Subscribed organization

If one of your organizations subscribes to PilotDuty Manager:

- the free limit of 3 flights per month no longer applies;
- you can turn on flight following in Manager;
- you can send feature ideas.

The organization's subscription doesn't include PilotDuty+ or PilotDuty Pro. See [Subscriptions](/en/docs/app/subscriptions).

The app has no booking features: bookings are made in Manager.
