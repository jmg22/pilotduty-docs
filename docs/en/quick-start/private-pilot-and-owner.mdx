This guide helps you get started with PilotDuty. Whether you fly alone or share an aircraft, you'll learn to record your flights and manage your aircraft at your own pace.

- **Start with the app.** After the welcome flow, set up your pilot profile and aircraft. Then learn about automatic flight recording, reviewing flight details and your electronic logbook.
- **Explore PilotDuty Manager.** The Manager lets you keep your aircraft details up to date and track its maintenance. It runs in a web browser, with the same account as the app: both share the same base. Members, bookings and billing can be added when you need them.

Start simple. Add features at your own pace.

# 1. The PilotDuty app

Setting up and using the iPhone app.

## 1.1 Update your pilot profile

Profile, ratings and initial flight time.

### Step 1: Open Settings

From the PilotDuty dashboard, tap the gear icon at the bottom of the screen.

![The app dashboard, with a circle on the gear icon at the bottom right](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/profile-open-settings.png)

### Step 2: Open your pilot profile

Under Settings, select “Pilot Profile”.

![The app settings, with a circle on “Pilot Profile”](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/profile-pilot-profile.png)

### Step 3: Review your pilot profile

Update your name, pilot information, licences and ratings, applicable rules, and home time zone.

![The “Pilot Profile” screen: pilot information (blurred), “700 CARs” rules, time zone and licences](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/profile-review.png)

### Step 4: Hours and imports

Enter your initial flight time (“Initial flight time”). With PilotDuty+, import an electronic logbook or scan a paper logbook (“Import Logbook”). With PilotDuty Pro, subscribe to your airline's iCal feed (“Airline calendar sync”, in beta).

![The “Logbook” section of the profile: “Initial flight time”, “Import Logbook” and “Airline calendar sync”](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/profile-hours-imports.png)

See [Importing an existing logbook](/en/docs/app/logbook-import) and [Airline roster](/en/docs/app/airline-roster).

## 1.2 Add an aircraft

Create and review your personal aircraft profile.

### Step 1: Open the Aircraft tab

From the dashboard, tap the airplane icon in the bottom bar.

![The dashboard, with a circle on the airplane icon in the bottom bar](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/personal-aircraft-tab.png)

### Step 2: Tap the + button

In “Tracked Aircraft”, tap + in the upper-right corner to add an aircraft.

![The “Tracked Aircraft” list, with a circle on the + button in the upper-right corner](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/personal-aircraft-add-button.png)

### Step 3: Choose a method

For a personal aircraft, select “Create Manually”. “Adopt an iBeacon” links a beacon, and “Organization Aircraft” is for an organization's aircraft.

![The “Add an Aircraft” menu, with a circle on “Create Manually”](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/personal-aircraft-create-manually.png)

### Step 4: Choose the initiator

In “Edit Area”, set “Initiator” to “iBeacon” if the aircraft has a beacon, otherwise to “User initiated”. If needed, tap “Adopt an iBeacon” to link the beacon.

![The “Edit Area” screen, with a circle on “Initiator” set to “iBeacon”](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/personal-aircraft-initiator.png)

### Step 5: Complete the aircraft details

Enter the “CallSign” (registration) and “Aircraft Type”, then the relevant characteristics: takeoff speed, category, engine and propeller.

![The “Edit Area” screen: “CallSign”, “Aircraft Type”, takeoff speed, category, engine and propeller](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/personal-aircraft-details.png)

### Step 6: Go back and check

Tap the back arrow in the upper-left corner. The profile saves on its own: there is no Save button. Make sure the aircraft appears in “Tracked Aircraft”.

![The “Edit Area” screen, with a circle on the back arrow in the upper-left corner](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/personal-aircraft-back.png)

See [iBeacon](/en/docs/app/ibeacon).

## 1.3 Set up automatic flight tracking

Review permissions and flight tracking settings.

### Step 1: Open Settings

From the dashboard, tap the gear icon in the bottom bar.

![The dashboard, with a circle on the gear icon at the bottom right](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/tracking-open-settings.png)

### Step 2: Select General

In Settings, select “General”.

![The app settings, with a circle on “General”](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/tracking-general.png)

### Step 3: Check permissions

Under “Autorisation Status”, confirm that “Location” is set to “Always” and that notifications, “Motion” and “Camera” are granted (green). Resolve any yellow or red status in the iPhone settings.

![The “Autorisation Status” screen: “Location” set to “Always”, with “Notification”, “Motion” and “Camera” “Granted”](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/tracking-permissions.png)

### Step 4: Set the flight separation delay

In “General”, turn on “GPS Tracking” and set the “Parking time limit”, under “Flight overlap threshold”: 30 seconds suits most pilots, and 120 seconds is recommended for flight training organizations, as in the screenshot.

![The “General” settings: “GPS Tracking” on and “Parking time limit” at 120](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/tracking-parking-time-limit.png)

See [Setting the parking time limit](/en/docs/app/automatic-recording#setting-the-parking-time-limit).

### Step 5: Open Flight Following

Return to Settings, then select “Flight Following”.

![The app settings, with a circle on “Flight Following”](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/tracking-flight-following.png)

### Step 6: Turn on tracking in PilotDuty Manager

Under “Flight Following”, turn on “Enable flight Following on PilotDuty Manager”. Your organization can then follow your flights in PilotDuty Manager. Without a subscribed organization, this setting is turned off.

![The “Flight Following” settings, with a circle on the “Enable flight Following on PilotDuty Manager” switch](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/tracking-enable-manager.png)

See [Flight following](/en/docs/app/flight-following).

## 1.4 Automatic flight recording

From aircraft detection to flight approval, including editing and deleting.

### Step 1: Aircraft detection

Near the aircraft, PilotDuty automatically detects a flight event and displays its status.

![The dashboard with the “Flight Event Started” banner](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/recording-detection.png)

### Step 2: Flight status

The top banner shows the flight phase: event started (“Flight Event Started”), taxiing (“Taxiing”), flying (“Flying”) or parked (“Parked”).

![The dashboard with the “Taxiing” banner](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/recording-flight-status.png)

### Step 3: In-flight recording

At takeoff, a flight card appears with times and a progress bar.

![The dashboard with the “Flying” banner and the card of the flight in progress](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/recording-in-flight.png)

### Step 4: Landing notification

After landing, if flight following is turned on, tap the “Confirm safe” notification or reopen the app to confirm your safe arrival.

![The iPhone lock screen with the “Confirm safe” notification](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/recording-landing-notification.png)

### Step 5: Confirm safe arrival

In the confirmation prompt, tap “Yes” to confirm you landed safely.

![The “Confirmation” prompt: “Do you want to confirm safe landing?”, with “No” and “Yes”](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/recording-confirm-safe.png)

### Step 6: Review flight data

Review the recorded flight card and check the times, aircraft and airports before approval.

![The recorded flight card on the dashboard: times, CSY4 airports and date](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/recording-review.png)

### Step 7: Edit a flight

Tap the flight card to open its details (“Edit Flight”) and correct information as needed.

![The “Edit Flight” screen: aircraft, departure airport and times](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/recording-edit.png)

### Step 8: Approve the flight

On the dashboard, swipe the flight card right and tap “Approve”.

![The card swiped right, with the blue “Approve” button](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/recording-approve.png)

### Step 9: View the logbook

Open the “Logbook” tab to see today's flights or earlier entries.

![The “Logbook” tab: total flight time and today's flight](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/recording-logbook.png)

### Step 10: Unapprove a flight

In the logbook, swipe the flight card left and tap “UnApprove”. Then edit the flight on the dashboard and approve it again.

![In “Logbook”, the card swiped left, with the yellow “UnApprove” button](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/recording-unapprove.png)

### Step 11: Delete a flight

To remove an incorrect entry, swipe the flight card left on the dashboard and tap “Delete”.

![The card swiped left, with the red “Delete” button](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/recording-delete.png)

See [Reviewing and approving flights](/en/docs/app/review-and-approval).

# 2. PilotDuty Manager

Access the Manager and set up your organization.

## 2.1 Access the Manager and sign in

Open [PilotDuty Manager](https://manager.pilotduty.com) and sign in.

### Step 1: Open PilotDuty Manager from the app

In the PilotDuty app, open Settings, then tap “PilotDuty Manager”.

![The app settings, with a circle on “PilotDuty Manager”](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/manager-access-app-settings.png)

### Step 2: Review your organizations

Tap the “PilotDuty Manager” button to open the Manager website. This screen also shows which organizations your account is linked to.

![The app's “PilotDuty Manager” screen: the Manager button, the list of organizations and the account (names blurred)](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/manager-access-organizations.png)

See [With your organization](/en/docs/app/with-your-organization).

### Step 3: Open the Manager website

Go to manager.pilotduty.com. Select “Sign In” if you already have an account, or “Get Started” to create one.

![The manager.pilotduty.com home page on iPhone, with a circle on “Sign In” in the menu](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/manager-access-website.png)

### Step 4: Create your account, if needed

If you are new, follow the sign-up instructions: Google, Apple, or email and password. If you already have an account in the app, use the same credentials: the app and the Manager share the same base.

![The “Create an account” page: sign in with Google or Apple, or email and password](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/manager-access-create-account.png)

### Step 5: Open the organizations menu

Once signed in, open the three-line menu and tap “Your Organizations” to view or choose your organization.

![The Manager on iPhone, with a circle on the three-line menu](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/manager-access-menu.png)

### Step 6: Add a shortcut on iPhone

In Safari, tap Share, then “Show More” if needed, and select “Add to Home Screen”. On Android, use the browser menu and its equivalent option.

![Safari's Share menu, with “Add to Home Screen”](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/manager-access-share-sheet.png)

### Step 7: Confirm the shortcut

Name the shortcut, then tap “Add”. PilotDuty Manager appears on your home screen.

![The “Add to Home Screen” screen, with a circle on “Add” (shortcut name blurred)](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/manager-access-home-screen.png)

## 2.2 Create your organization

Create your account and your organization in the Manager. As long as your organization has a single member, you use it for free, with no subscription and no time limit.

### Step 1: Sign up

On the PilotDuty Manager home page, click “Sign Up” at the top right, or “Get Started”.

![The PilotDuty Manager home page, with an arrow to “Sign Up” at the top right](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/create-organization-sign-up.png)

### Step 2: Create an account

Choose Google or Apple, or enter your email address and password, then click “Get started”.

![The “Create an account” form: Google, Apple, email, password and “Get started” button](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/create-organization-account.png)

> **Important.** If you already have a PilotDuty account, do not create a new one. Click “Already have an account? Sign In” at the bottom of the screen, then sign in.

### Step 3: Describe your organization

The wizard has three steps: details, operations and done. In the details step, enter your organization's name, your full name, the address, phone and email, then go to the next step.

![The wizard's “Details” step in 2025: the organization name, Acme Aviation, and the “Continue” button](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/create-organization-name.png)

In 2025, this step only asked for the organization name, and the wizard had only two steps.

### Step 4: Finish

In the operations step, choose at least one activity, your country and your main airport. A screen then confirms that your organization is created and active. Click the button to start the setup.

![The wizard's final screen in 2025, “You're all set!”, with the “Continue to your Dashboard” button](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/create-organization-complete.png)

In 2025, this screen showed “You're all set!” and the “Continue to your Dashboard” button.

See [Getting started with PilotDuty Manager](/en/docs/manager/getting-started).

## 2.3 Add and manage an aircraft

Register your aircraft and track its maintenance.

### Step 1: Open Aircrafts

In PilotDuty Manager, open the three-line menu at the top left, then select “Aircrafts”.

![The Manager menu, with “Aircrafts”](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/manager-aircraft-menu.png)

### Step 2: Add an aircraft

At the top of the “Aircrafts” page, tap “New aircraft”.

![The “Aircrafts” list, with the “New aircraft” button at the top](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/manager-aircraft-new.png)

### Step 3: Complete the seven steps

Complete the seven-step wizard. Some details can be added later, but we recommend completing everything now.

![The “New aircraft” wizard, step 1 of 7 “Identity”: registration, ICAO type, make and model](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/manager-aircraft-wizard.png)

### Step 4: Edit the profile

To edit an existing aircraft, open “Aircrafts” and use the three-dot menu to the left of its name. You can update its profile, including hours and maintenance details.

![The “Aircrafts” list, with the three-dot menu to the left of each aircraft](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/manager-aircraft-edit.png)

### Step 5: Review the aircraft details

After creating the aircraft, open its details (“Aircraft details”) and check airframe time, cycles and maintenance tables.

![The “Aircraft details” page: “Overview”, “Snags” and “MEL” tabs, airframe time and cycles](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/manager-aircraft-details.png)

### Step 6: Manage inspections

Create an initial inspection for each check, for example the 100-hour and the 50-hour: it sets the starting point for the next due dates. When an inspection is due, start it and give the work order to your maintenance shop.

![An inspection tile with no history, with the “Create Initial Inspection” button](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/manager-aircraft-inspections.png)

See [Fleet and maintenance](/en/docs/manager/fleet-and-maintenance).

> Once the aircraft is created in PilotDuty Manager, you or an invited member can add it in the app: open the Aircraft tab, tap +, then select “Organization Aircraft”. There is no need to create the aircraft profile again by hand.

## 2.4 Invite members

Give other users access to your organization. To invite members, your organization needs an active subscription. See [Subscription](/en/docs/manager/subscription).

### Step 1: Open Settings

In the three-line menu, select “Settings”.

![The Manager menu, with “Settings”](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/invite-members-settings.png)

### Step 2: Select Members

Under the “Organization” tab, open the dropdown and select “Members”.

![The “Organization” tab of the settings, with the dropdown and “Members”](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/invite-members-members.png)

### Step 3: Send the invitation

Click “Invite Members”. Enter the email, choose the role (Pilot by default) and the invitation language, then click “Send Invites”.

![The “Invite Members” form: email, “Pilot” role, invitation language and “Send Invites” button](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/invite-members-form.png)

### Step 4: Accept the invitation

The member receives an invitation email, valid for 7 days:

1. they open the email;
2. they accept the invitation;
3. they sign in to PilotDuty, or create their account, with the invited address.

They then join your organization. See [Members and roles](/en/docs/manager/members-and-roles).

## 3.2 Add a flight manually

Enter and review a flight in the Manager.

### Step 1: Select Pilots

From the main menu, select “Pilots” to open your pilot record.

![The Manager main menu, with a circle on “Pilots”](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/manual-flight-menu.png)

### Step 2: Open the Flights tab

In your pilot record, select the “Flights” tab.

![The pilot record, with a circle on the “Flights” tab](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/manual-flight-flights-tab.png)

### Step 3: Show the flight list

In the dropdown, choose “Flight list” to see recorded flights.

![The “Flights” tab of the pilot record, with its dropdown](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/manual-flight-list.png)

### Step 4: Tap Add Flight

In the flight list, tap “+ Add Flight”.

![The flight list, with a circle on the “+ Add Flight” button](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/manual-flight-add-button.png)

### Step 5: Enter flight details

Enter the aircraft, airports, times and all required details. All four times are required. Review, then tap “Add”.

![The “Add Flight” window: aircraft, call sign, type, airports and times](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/manual-flight-form.png)

### Step 6: Check the flight sheet

Return to “Flightsheet”. If there is a booking for the same aircraft at the same time, the flight is linked to it automatically. Otherwise, it is not linked to any booking.

![The Manager main menu, with a circle on “Flightsheet”](/docs-media/en/quick-start/private-pilot-and-owner/manual-flight-flightsheet.png)

See [Pilots and duty time](/en/docs/manager/pilots-and-duty-time).
